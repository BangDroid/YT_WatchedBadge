 # YT_WatchedBadge
Adds a "Watched" cover to any watched videos on YouTube. Includes options for progress bar configuration: Hide the progress bar alltogether (the previous version behavior); hide only completed progress bars, or just obscure them.

Now including a build in SVG image for the Watched Cover, saving size and memory and better scaling at various thumbnail sizes.
The progress bar is now retained allowing easy visual tracking of progress. For the purposes of this userstyle, we consider a video with **at least 85% progress** as watched, explained in [this issue](https://github.com/BangDroid/YT_WatchedBadge/issues/2).

Compatible with YouTube's own light and dark themes, no guaranteed compatibility for other userstyle custom themes. 

Occasionally a video will be attributed 100% watch progress when only a small amount has actually been watched, and even instances where the progrss bar is full on videos that are not watched at all and don't even exist in the history. Subsequently, the Watched Cover is misappropriately applied. It's an issue on YouTube's end, there's nothing we can do about it.
